Section 01

Scope & Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android mobile application Photo Recovery & Restore (in-app brand name PhotoRecover) handles information on your device.

We respect your privacy and adhere strictly to Google Play Store Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy, Permissions and APIs that Access Sensitive Information Policy, and All Files Access Policy. Because Photo Recovery & Restore operates completely offline on your phone, we do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data or media files.

Section 02

Information Accessed On-Device & Why

When—and only when—you initiate a photo recovery scan, Smart Cleanup check, or Recovery Shield action, the app reads image files and associated file metadata stored on your device solely to perform the requested feature:

  • Image Files & Binary Headers: Inspected locally to verify valid image signatures (such as JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF, GIF, BMP, and DNG/RAW headers), decode bounded thumbnails for preview, and extract recoverable embedded images from accessible cache or thumbnail containers.
  • File & Media Metadata: Includes file names, byte sizes, pixel dimensions, modification/creation timestamps, storage paths, Android MediaStore IDs, trash status flags, content:// URIs, and local SHA-256 content digests.
  • Device Storage Capacity Metrics: Total and available internal storage bytes read via Android’s standard StatFs API solely to display the storage usage summary bar on the Home screen.
Transparent Recovery Accuracy: A file’s absence from Android’s gallery index is never treated as proof that it was recently deleted. Photo Recovery & Restore distinguishes Android system-trash items from accessible cached, thumbnail, messaging, and residual storage copies (Possible Recovery) and does not claim guaranteed recovery of storage blocks that have already been overwritten by the operating system.
Section 03

Android Permissions Explained (Android 8 – Android 16)

Photo Recovery & Restore requests only the Android permissions required for its core photo recovery, restoration, and cleanup functionality across Android 8.0 (API 26) through Android 16 (API 36):

  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+): Allows the app to read accessible gallery images so it can avoid showing existing library photos as deleted candidates and run Smart Cleanup (Duplicate, Old, and Large Photos).
  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED (Android 14+): Supports Android’s granular Selected Photos access mode when you choose to grant access only to specific images.
  • android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 8–12, maxSdkVersion="32") & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 8–9, maxSdkVersion="28"): Required on legacy Android versions to scan shared storage images and save restored photo copies into Pictures/Photo Recovery & Restore.
  • android.permission.MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (All Files Access, Android 11+): Requested only after showing a clear in-app explanation and directing you to Android’s official system permission screen. This permission enables the one-click recovery engine to locate recoverable image files stored outside standard MediaStore indexes—such as hidden directories, residual app caches, thumbnail stores, and unindexed shared-storage folders.
Strict Permission Safeguards: We never use storage permissions in the background without user initiation, never modify or delete files during a recovery scan, never request root privileges, and never access other applications’ private internal sandboxes (/data/data). You may revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Photo Recovery & Restore > Permissions.
Section 04

Recovery Shield, Restored Photos & Smart Cleanup

Recovery Shield (Private Local Backup)

When you explicitly select photos to protect with Recovery Shield, the app stores a verified copy and its SHA-256 integrity record inside the app’s private internal storage directory on your device. Android Auto Backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false"), ensuring protected copies are never silently uploaded to cloud backup servers.

Saving Recovered Photos ("Save a copy")

Restoring a photo runs only when you tap restore/save. It writes a verified new copy into shared storage (Pictures/Photo Recovery & Restore via Android MediaStore, or a folder you explicitly authorize) and never deletes or alters the original source file. To show your Recovered Photos history screen, the app stores lightweight local metadata (restored content:// URI, display name, MIME type, source label, and timestamp)—not duplicate raw image bytes.

Smart Cleanup (Duplicate, Old & Large Photos)

Smart Cleanup analyzes accessible library photos locally on your phone. No photo is ever deleted automatically; removal occurs strictly when you select specific items and confirm Android’s system deletion prompt.

Section 05

Data Storage, Retention & How to Delete Your Data

Because Photo Recovery & Restore maintains no user accounts and operates no external backend servers, 100% of app state resides locally on your Android device under clear retention bounds:

  • Active Scan Results: Held in volatile device memory (RAM) while viewing scan results and released when closed.
  • Bounded Inspection Metadata Cache: To speed up repeat scans, the app stores a size-capped local cache containing relative file identifiers, byte size, modification time, and detected image dimensions. Entries automatically expire after 14 days.
  • Temporary Carved-Image Cache: Bounded temporary files created inside the app’s private cache directory during embedded-image inspection are automatically pruned by age and size limits.
  • Preferences, History & Recovery Shield: Stored locally on your device until you clear or remove them.

How You Can Delete All App Data Immediately

  • Remove Protected Copies: Open Recovery Shield (or Settings > Recovery Shield > Clear protected copies) inside the app.
  • Erase All App Data & Caches: Open Android Settings > Apps > Photo Recovery & Restore > Storage & cache and tap Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall the application. This permanently deletes all app-private preferences, caches, history records, and Recovery Shield files from your device. (Photos you previously saved to your public Gallery remain safely in your Gallery unless you delete them there.)
Section 06

Data Sharing, Third-Party Services & Security

We do not share, sell, rent, trade, or transfer your photos, media metadata, or personal information to any third party.

  • No Cloud Servers: No developer-operated server receives your images, file lists, or usage activity.
  • No Third-Party Ad or Analytics SDKs: The app contains no advertising SDKs, crash-reporting trackers that upload personal data, or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • Explicit User Intents Only: When you tap Rate us (opens Google Play Store) or Share app (opens Android’s system share sheet to share the app’s Google Play link), Android launches the corresponding system app on your device under that platform’s own privacy terms.
